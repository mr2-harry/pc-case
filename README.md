Case design / inspo
I've always really loved the design of the early 2000s cases, I like how vibrant and different they were. Back then, they had to compete over what is coolest and there were certainly no rules, and they certainly did not know what consumers liked. I think this lead to companies letting designers and engineers have a lot of freedom, throw a shot in the dark every now and then, and it produced, in my opinion, some of the most beautiful PC cases in any era.
This case will pull heavily from the Alienwares between 2004-2010 as these were when I think they truly had incerdible designs, however, I intend for it to be modernised, and have good airflow. I am also a fan of large cases, with a lot of room for 5.25 inch bays, hard drives, water blocks, radiators etc.
The case is intended to be LARGE, so there is more than plenty room to stuff anything you want in there.

I think I will ideally have 3 5.25 inch bays, so theres room for:
    -Blu-Ray, DVD, CD burner
    -Blu-Ray, DVD, CD reader
    -SD Card / multi port reader

Front panel / door with 4 knobs, 2-4 switches (Look up old alienware PC Case, front had knobs for fan control)
Fan control will be unnessecary, will be handled by software, so knobs / switches will be used for light modes, speeds, colours etc
Front panel with 4 usb3.2gen2 (or whatever they've called it now, eyeroll) ports, 2 usb C ports, headphone jack, mic jack.

Front panel will most likely need to be designed by myself too, along with possible the usb panel. Cross that bridge when we come to it...
